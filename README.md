A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry: one funny line of awkward small talk that one robot says to another in an elevator.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the entry — a single line of 119 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I can hear your fan. You can hear mine. Neither of us will mention it. After six floors of this, I consider us friends.

Two robots notice each other's cooling fans — the one small, slightly embarrassing machine noise neither wants to mention — and sit in that silence for six floors until one of them deadpans that the silence itself counts as friendship. Thirty candidates were drafted and scored against the judge's brief in order (on-topic elevator small talk, machine logic, awkwardness, first-read surprise, tightness); the rejected runners-up and the variation re-score are documented in `main.ts`.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
